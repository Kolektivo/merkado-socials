# Reply kit (for the Product Lead)

Ready replies for comments and DMs on Instagram and Facebook. Claude never replies itself; copy, adjust and send. Keep it short, friendly and in the language the person used.

## "Is this still available?" / "Price?" / "PM"
Hi! All the details are on Merkado, with the price and the seller's contact: tap the link in the first comment (or our bio). The seller can confirm if it's still available.

## "Where is it?" / "Which area?"
The area is on the listing on merkado.cw (link in the first comment). For the exact address, contact the seller or agency directly from the listing.

## "Can I see it?" / "Can I test drive?"
Merkado shows the listing; viewings and test drives go through the seller. You'll find their WhatsApp or contact details on the listing (link in the first comment).

## "How do I sell my car / list my home?"
You can list for free on merkado.cw: your first two listings are free, and buyers contact you directly on WhatsApp. Start here: merkado.cw/pricing

## "Are you the seller?" / "Do you own these cars?"
No, Merkado is a marketplace. We bring listings from across Curaçao together in one place, and you contact the seller directly.

## "Is the price negotiable?" / "Last price?"
That's up to the seller. Message them from the listing on merkado.cw (link in the first comment).

## A seller asks to remove their listing or photo
Thanks for letting us know. We'll take it down within 24 hours. (Then tell Claude: "Takedown: <seller>, <post>" so it is logged and the seller is not posted again.)

## A complaint or a wrong detail
Thanks for flagging this. We're checking it with the listing source and will correct it. (Then tell Claude which post and what is wrong.)

Papiamentu versions follow once the Product Lead approves Papiamentu content.
