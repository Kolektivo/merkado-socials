# 01 Goals, audience and strategy

## Goal

Make Merkado the first place people on Curaçao look when they want a car or a home, and the first place dealers, agencies and private sellers want their listing to appear.

Launch campaign line (already on merkado.cw): **"Made to search, not scroll."** On Curaçao, finding a car or a home means scrolling Facebook groups full of "Price?", "PM" and "Still available??". Merkado is the fix: listings from across the island in one place, with the price, the details and the seller right there.

| Metric (Metricool) | Baseline 22 Oct 2026 | Target by 20 Dec 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram followers | 13 | 150 |
| Facebook Page followers | 4 | 150 |
| Avg reach per feed post (IG and FB each) | none yet | 300 |

Followers are a means. Reach to people on the island, shares and saves, and clicks to listings are what count.

## Audience

- People on Curaçao looking for a car or a home (buy or rent), mostly on Facebook, many already in buy-and-sell groups. Price-sensitive: they love a bargain (Product Lead, 28 Oct 2026).
- People in the Netherlands planning a move, a stay or a holiday home on Curaçao.
- Dealers and real-estate agencies, who should want their stock on Merkado.
- Private sellers who could list for free.

## Strategy (29 Oct 2026)

### Positioning
Merkado is the island's search engine for cars and homes: clear prices, real listings, the seller named, no "PM". Social media shows that every week with real listings, and makes people come to merkado.cw to search, not to scroll a feed.

### Brand on social
- **Look:** Merkado violet and paper, Inter Display headlines, rounded photo frames, the oversized .cw tag as the one decorative shape. Feed alternates paper and violet so the grid reads as a checkerboard. No stickers, gradients, stock-photo clichés or AI-generated listings.
- **Voice:** a helpful neighbour who knows the market. Short, plain, local, factual. Prices and dates always. No hype words, no hashtags, no em dashes.
- **Trust:** every listing credits its seller, links to the listing, and is checked at the source before it posts. Private sellers are never named. AI is never used to change a car or a home.

### Content pillars
1. **Listings (about 70%):** weekly car and real estate carousels and two stories, bargains first, themes rotating. This is the product, shown.
2. **Market knowledge (about 15%):** Island Index data and Before you buy guides. Saves and shares come from useful posts.
3. **Brand and campaign (about 10%):** The comment section, "Made to search, not scroll.", unique posts for launches and island moments.
4. **Sellers (about 5%):** List on Merkado, free listings, WhatsApp contact. Sellers bring the listings that bring the buyers.

### What the platforms reward (checked 29 Oct 2026)
- Instagram says sends/shares and saves are among its strongest ranking signals; Explore leans on likes, saves and shares. Carousels earn time on post and saves. So posts are built to be saved and sent ("send this to someone looking for a car").
- Reels are the main route to people who do not follow us yet (Trial Reels test with non-followers first). We post no video today: the biggest growth gap (see Experiments).
- Instagram search (and Google, for public professional accounts) reads the profile name, bio, captions and alt text. Hence keywords in sentences, full alt text, and a keyword-rich display name.
- Facebook: organic Page reach is low (single-digit percent of followers); comments, Reels and a correct Page category help. 3 to 5 posts a week is the right range.

### Growth levers, in order
1. Consistency: 3 feed posts and 2 stories a week, every week.
2. Shareable bargains: price drops and "under XCG X" posts are made to be sent to a friend.
3. Profile set-up: keyword display name, clear bio with a tracked link, three pinned posts (launch post, List on Merkado, Island map), story Highlights (Cars, Homes, Bargains, Guides, Sell).
4. Comments and DMs answered fast by the Product Lead, using the reply kit (`captions/reply-kit.md`).
5. Experiments (one at a time, approved by the Product Lead): short Reels made from the carousels; Instagram collab posts with dealers who opt in; a small paid boost behind the best organic post of the month; Papiamentu captions.

### Measurement
Weekly: reach, saves, shares, comments, link clicks, profile visits and follower growth per post and per network; which theme, format and ground did best; one change for next week (docs/11-learnings.md). Monthly: report to the Product Lead with the top posts and the next month's focus.

### Risks and how they are handled
- Sold or wrong listings: pre-flight at the source when drafted and again within 48 hours; daily pull and replace.
- A seller asks to be removed: remove within 24 hours, log in `state/takedowns.md`.
- Data errors on merkado.cw (wrong year, missing mileage, sold listings still active): skipped and reported to the merkado-cw team.
- Over-claiming: no "every", "best", "cheapest" without a dated fact behind it.
